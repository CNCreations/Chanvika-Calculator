Discount Calculator

Calculate the discount amount and final price after applying a percentage discount to an original price.

What Is a Discount?

A discount is a reduction from the original price of a product or service. Discounts are commonly offered during sales, promotions, festivals, clearance events and special offers.

A percentage discount tells you how much of the original price is reduced.

Discount Formula

The discount amount can be calculated using:

Discount Amount = Original Price × Discount Percentage ÷ 100

The final price is:

Final Price = Original Price − Discount Amount

Discount Calculation Example

Suppose a product has an original price of ₹1,000 and a discount of 20%.

Discount Amount = ₹1,000 × 20% = ₹200

Final Price = ₹1,000 − ₹200 = ₹800

Therefore, after a 20% discount, you would pay ₹800.

How to Use This Discount Calculator

  1. Enter the original price of the product or service.
  2. Enter the discount percentage.
  3. Click Calculate.
  4. View the discount amount and final price.

How to Calculate a Sale Price

When a shop offers a percentage discount, first calculate the discount amount and then subtract it from the original price.

For example, a ₹2,500 product with a 15% discount has a discount amount of ₹375. The final price would therefore be ₹2,125.

Things to Check Before Accepting a Discount

  • Check the original price before applying the discount.
  • Confirm whether the advertised discount applies to the product you want.
  • Check whether taxes or other charges are added separately.
  • Compare the final price with other sellers when appropriate.
  • Some offers may have minimum purchase requirements or other conditions.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate a percentage discount?

Multiply the original price by the discount percentage and divide the result by 100.

What is 20% off ₹1,000?

A 20% discount on ₹1,000 is ₹200, making the final price ₹800.

Can the discount be 100%?

Yes. A 100% discount means the calculated final price is ₹0, assuming no additional charges apply.

Can I enter a decimal discount?

Yes. You can enter values such as 12.5% if the offer uses a decimal percentage.

Does this calculator include GST or other taxes?

No. This calculator only applies the discount to the original price. Taxes, fees or other charges should be considered separately if applicable.
