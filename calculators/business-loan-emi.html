Business Loan EMI Calculator

Estimate monthly EMI, total interest and repayment for a business loan.

Results are estimates for general informational purposes and may differ from actual financial, tax, lender or business outcomes.

What Does This Calculator Do?

Use this calculator to estimate repayment for a business loan before comparing lenders or financing options.

Formula

EMI = P × r × (1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n − 1).

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter the required values.
  2. Check that your inputs are reasonable and use the correct units.
  3. Click Calculate to view the estimate.
  4. Adjust the inputs to compare different scenarios.

Example

Example: A ₹10,00,000 loan at 11% p.a. for 5 years produces an estimated monthly EMI and total repayment based on the standard EMI formula.

Important Notes
