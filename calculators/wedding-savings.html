Wedding Savings Plan Calculator

Calculate monthly savings needed to reach a wedding budget by a target date.

This calculator provides a general estimate for planning and educational purposes. Verify important financial, tax, business or professional decisions with the relevant official source or qualified professional.

Formula

Monthly saving = (Target − Already saved) ÷ Months remaining

Example

₹6 lakh target, ₹1 lakh saved and 10 months left needs ₹50,000/month.

How to Use

  1. Enter the values relevant to your situation.
  2. Check units and rates before calculating.
  3. Click Calculate.
  4. Compare different scenarios by changing the inputs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are the results exact?

No. Results depend on your inputs and, where applicable, real-world rates, rules, fees and assumptions.

Can I use this for planning?

Yes. It is designed as a quick planning and estimation tool.
