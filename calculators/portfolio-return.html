Portfolio Return Calculator

Calculate weighted portfolio return from investment amounts and returns.

This calculator provides a general estimate for planning and educational purposes. Verify important financial, tax, business or professional decisions with the relevant official source or qualified professional.

Formula

Portfolio return = Σ(amount × return) ÷ Total amount

Example

A weighted portfolio return reflects how much capital is allocated to each asset.

How to Use

  1. Enter the values relevant to your situation.
  2. Check units and rates before calculating.
  3. Click Calculate.
  4. Compare different scenarios by changing the inputs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Yes. It is designed as a quick planning and estimation tool.
