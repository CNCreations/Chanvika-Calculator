Profit Margin Calculator

Calculate your profit or loss and profit margin instantly using the cost price and selling price of a product or service.

Profit margin is calculated against the selling price. A negative profit means a loss.

What Is Profit Margin?

Profit margin shows how much profit you earn from a sale compared with the selling price. It is commonly used by shop owners, retailers, wholesalers and small businesses to understand the profitability of their products or services.

For example, if you buy a product for ₹500 and sell it for ₹700, your profit is ₹200. The profit margin tells you what percentage of the ₹700 selling price represents profit.

Profit Margin Formula

The basic formulas are:

Profit = Selling Price − Cost Price

Profit Margin = (Profit ÷ Selling Price) × 100

If the selling price is lower than the cost price, the result is a loss and the profit margin will be negative.

Profit Margin Calculation Example

Suppose you purchase a product for ₹500 and sell it for ₹700.

Profit = ₹700 − ₹500 = ₹200

Profit Margin = (₹200 ÷ ₹700) × 100 = 28.57%

Therefore, your profit is ₹200 and your profit margin is approximately 28.57%.

Profit Margin Tips for Small Businesses

  • Keep track of your actual purchase cost before setting a selling price.
  • Remember that expenses such as packaging, delivery and other business costs can reduce your actual net profit.
  • Compare margins across products to identify which products are more profitable.
  • Do not confuse profit margin with markup percentage.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good profit margin?

There is no single profit margin that is ideal for every business. The appropriate margin depends on the industry, product, operating expenses, competition and pricing strategy.

Is profit margin calculated on cost price or selling price?

Profit margin is generally calculated using the selling price as the denominator. Markup, on the other hand, is calculated using the cost price.

What happens when the selling price is lower than the cost price?

You make a loss. The calculator will show a negative profit and a negative profit margin.

Does profit margin include other business expenses?

This calculator calculates the difference between cost price and selling price. Additional expenses such as rent, salaries, delivery charges, packaging and taxes may need to be considered separately when calculating overall business profitability.
