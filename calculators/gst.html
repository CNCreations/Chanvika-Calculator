GST Calculator

Calculate GST amount, total price including GST, and the base price from a GST-inclusive amount.

GST rates depend on the product/service and applicable rules. Verify the correct GST rate before invoicing, filing or making tax decisions.

What Is GST?

GST stands for Goods and Services Tax. It is an indirect tax applied to the supply of many goods and services in India.

The applicable GST rate can vary depending on the goods or services involved and the applicable tax rules.

GST Calculation Formula

When the entered amount is the price before GST:

GST Amount = Base Amount × GST Rate ÷ 100

The price including GST is:

Total Price = Base Amount + GST Amount

If the entered amount already includes GST, the base amount can be estimated using:

Base Amount = GST-Inclusive Amount ÷ (1 + GST Rate ÷ 100)

GST Calculation Example

Suppose the base price of a product is ₹1,000 and the applicable GST rate is 18%.

GST = ₹1,000 × 18% = ₹180

Total Price = ₹1,000 + ₹180 = ₹1,180

Therefore, the customer would pay ₹1,180 if no other charges apply.

How to Calculate GST From an Inclusive Price

If a product's final price is ₹1,180 including 18% GST, the estimated base price is:

₹1,180 ÷ 1.18 = ₹1,000

The GST component is therefore approximately: ₹180.

CGST and SGST

For eligible intra-state supplies, GST may be divided into CGST (Central GST) and SGST (State GST).

For example, where an applicable GST rate of 18% is split equally, it may be represented as 9% CGST and 9% SGST.

The actual tax treatment depends on the nature and location of the supply and applicable rules.

What Is IGST?

IGST stands for Integrated Goods and Services Tax. It generally applies to eligible inter-state supplies and certain other transactions under India's GST framework.

Always verify the applicable tax treatment for the specific transaction.

Important GST Information

This calculator performs a mathematical GST calculation based on the rate entered by the user. It does not determine which GST rate applies to a particular product or service.

GST rules, rates, exemptions, classification and other tax requirements can change. Businesses should verify the applicable rules before preparing invoices or filing tax returns.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is GST calculated?

GST is calculated by multiplying the taxable base amount by the applicable GST rate and dividing by 100.

What is 18% GST on ₹1,000?

18% GST on ₹1,000 is ₹180. The total including GST is ₹1,180.

How do I remove GST from an inclusive price?

Divide the GST-inclusive amount by 1 plus the GST rate divided by 100. For example, at 18% GST, divide the inclusive amount by 1.18.

Does this calculator decide the correct GST rate?

No. You must enter the applicable GST rate yourself. The calculator does not classify goods or services for tax purposes.

Is this GST Calculator suitable for invoices?

It can be used for quick mathematical calculations, but businesses should verify the applicable GST rate and tax treatment before issuing an official invoice.

Yes. You can use the calculator for free.
