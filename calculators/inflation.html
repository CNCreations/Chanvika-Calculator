Inflation Calculator

Estimate how much today's money or an expense could cost in the future based on an assumed annual inflation rate.

This is a mathematical estimate based on a constant annual inflation rate. Actual inflation can vary over time and across products, services and locations.

What Is Inflation?

Inflation is the general increase in the prices of goods and services over time. As prices rise, the purchasing power of the same amount of money generally decreases.

For example, if an item costs ₹1,000 today and its price increases consistently over several years, you may need more than ₹1,000 in the future to purchase the same item.

Inflation Calculator Formula

The estimated future cost is calculated using:

Future Cost = Present Cost × (1 + Inflation Rate)Years

The annual inflation rate is converted into a decimal before applying the formula.

Inflation Calculation Example

Suppose something costs ₹1,00,000 today and you assume an average annual inflation rate of 6% for 10 years.

Using the compound inflation formula, the estimated future cost would be approximately:

₹1,79,085.85

This means the estimated increase would be approximately: ₹79,085.85.

This does not predict the actual future price. It simply demonstrates what would happen if inflation remained at 6% every year.

Inflation and Purchasing Power

Inflation affects purchasing power because the same amount of money may buy fewer goods and services in the future.

For example, if prices increase significantly over a long period, ₹1,00,000 saved today may not have the same purchasing power several years from now.

This is one reason inflation is an important consideration when planning long-term savings, investments and future expenses.

Why Long-Term Inflation Matters

Even a relatively moderate inflation rate can significantly affect prices over long periods because inflation compounds year after year.

For example, an assumed 6% annual inflation rate does not mean that prices increase by only 60% after ten years. The calculation compounds each year's increase on the previous year's amount.

How to Use This Inflation Calculator

  1. Enter the current price or amount.
  2. Enter an assumed annual inflation rate.
  3. Enter the number of years.
  4. Click Calculate.
  5. View the estimated future cost and increase.

Important Information

Inflation does not necessarily remain constant every year. Different categories such as food, education, housing, healthcare and fuel can experience different rates of price change.

Therefore, this calculator should be treated as a planning estimate, not a prediction of actual future prices.

For financial planning, consider using multiple inflation assumptions rather than relying on a single rate.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does an inflation rate mean?

An inflation rate represents the assumed annual percentage increase in prices used for the calculation.

How does inflation affect money?

When prices rise, the purchasing power of a fixed amount of money generally decreases.

Does this calculator predict actual inflation?

No. It calculates an estimate based on the constant annual inflation rate entered by the user.

Why does inflation compound?

Each year's assumed increase is applied to the previous year's amount, which causes the estimated future cost to compound over time.

Can I use this for retirement planning?

Yes, it can be useful for estimating how future expenses might change. However, actual retirement planning should consider changing inflation, investment returns, taxes and other financial factors.
