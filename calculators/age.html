Age Calculator

Calculate your exact completed age in years, months and days using your date of birth and a chosen calculation date.

What Is an Age Calculator?

An age calculator helps you find the completed age of a person from their date of birth up to a selected date. Instead of manually counting the years, months and days between two dates, this calculator performs the calculation automatically.

You can use it to calculate your current age or find out how old someone will be on a particular date.

How Does the Age Calculator Work?

The calculator compares the date of birth with the selected calculation date. It determines the completed years first and then calculates the remaining months and days.

The calculation takes the different lengths of months into account, including February and leap years.

Age Calculation Example

Suppose a person was born on 15 January 2000 and the calculation date is 15 August 2026.

Their completed age would be:

26 years, 7 months, 0 days

You can use the calculator above to calculate the age between any two dates.

How to Use This Age Calculator

  1. Enter the person's date of birth.
  2. Select the date on which you want to calculate the age.
  3. Click the Calculate button.
  4. View the completed age in years, months and days.

What Can You Use an Age Calculator For?

  • Checking your current age.
  • Calculating someone's age on a specific date.
  • Checking age eligibility for applications or events.
  • Calculating age for birthdays and personal records.
  • Finding the exact difference between a birth date and another date.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I calculate my age on a future date?

Yes. Select your date of birth and then choose any future date in the "Calculate Age On" field.

Does the calculator show age in months and days?

Yes. The calculator displays completed years, months and days between the two selected dates.

Does the calculator account for leap years?

Yes. The date calculation uses the calendar dates and accounts for differences in month lengths, including leap years.

What happens if the date of birth is after the calculation date?

The calculator will show an error because the date of birth cannot be later than the date on which the age is being calculated.

Is this calculator free?

Yes. The Age Calculator is free to use.
