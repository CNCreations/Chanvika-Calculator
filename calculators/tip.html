Tip Calculator

Calculate the tip amount, total bill and how much each person should pay.

The tip percentage is calculated on the bill amount before the tip is added. This calculator does not include taxes or service charges.

What Is a Tip Calculator?

A tip calculator helps you quickly calculate how much tip to leave on a restaurant, café, hotel or other service bill.

It can also divide the final amount equally between multiple people.

How to Calculate a Tip

The basic formula is:

Tip = Bill Amount × Tip Percentage ÷ 100

The total bill is:

Total Bill = Bill Amount + Tip

Tip Calculator Example

Suppose a restaurant bill is ₹1,500 and you want to leave a 10% tip for 3 people.

The tip would be ₹150.

The total bill would be ₹1,650.

If the bill is divided equally, each person would pay ₹550.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter the bill amount.
  2. Enter the desired tip percentage.
  3. Enter the number of people sharing the bill.
  4. Click Calculate.
  5. Review the tip and payment amount.

Tip Percentage Examples

  • 5% — small tip
  • 10% — common basic tip calculation
  • 15% — moderate tip
  • 20% — generous tip

Tip customs can vary by country, business and situation. Check whether a service charge has already been included in the bill before adding an additional tip.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the tip calculated before or after tax?

This calculator calculates the tip on the bill amount you enter. If your entered bill already includes taxes, the tip will be calculated on that amount.

Can I split the bill between multiple people?

Yes. Enter the number of people and the calculator will show the equal amount each person needs to pay.

Does this calculator include service charges?

No. Service charges are not automatically included. Enter the amount you want to use as the bill amount.

Can I use a 0% tip?

Yes. A 0% tip will simply return the original bill amount as the total.

Is this Tip Calculator free?

Yes. The calculator is free to use.
