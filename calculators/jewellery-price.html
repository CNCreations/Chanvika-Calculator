Jewellery Price Calculator

Estimate the price of jewellery using metal weight, metal rate, wastage, making charges and GST.

Jewellery pricing varies by purity, metal rate, wastage, making charges, taxes and jeweller. This calculator provides an estimate only. Verify the final invoice before making a purchase.

How Is Jewellery Price Calculated?

Jewellery pricing can include several components. These may include the value of the precious metal, wastage charges, making charges and applicable taxes.

The exact pricing method can differ between jewellers and jewellery products, so the calculator should be treated as an estimate rather than an official quotation.

Jewellery Price Calculation Formula

The basic metal value is calculated as:

Metal Value = Weight × Metal Rate

Estimated wastage value is calculated as:

Wastage Value = Metal Value × Wastage % ÷ 100

In this calculator, making charges are calculated on the metal value plus the estimated wastage value:

Making Charge = (Metal Value + Wastage Value) × Making % ÷ 100

The taxable subtotal is:

Subtotal = Metal Value + Wastage Value + Making Charge

Finally:

GST = Subtotal × GST % ÷ 100

Estimated Total = Subtotal + GST

Jewellery Price Calculation Example

Suppose you are purchasing jewellery with the following values:

  • Weight: 10 grams
  • Metal rate: ₹6,500/gram
  • Wastage: 3%
  • Making charge: 10%
  • GST: 3%

Metal Value = 10 × ₹6,500 = ₹65,000

Wastage Value = 3% of ₹65,000 = ₹1,950

Making Charge = 10% of ₹66,950 = ₹6,695

Subtotal = ₹73,645

GST at 3% = ₹2,209.35

Estimated Total = ₹75,854.35

Jewellery Purity and Metal Rate

Precious-metal jewellery may be sold in different purities. The applicable metal rate can therefore depend on the purity and the market rate used by the jeweller.

Always confirm whether the quoted rate is for the actual purity of the jewellery you are purchasing.

What Is Wastage Charge?

Wastage is commonly used to represent an additional amount associated with the manufacturing process. The way it is calculated can vary between jewellers and products.

Therefore, the wastage calculation in this tool is a simplified estimation method.

What Are Making Charges?

Making charges represent the cost associated with designing, manufacturing and finishing jewellery.

Jewellers may calculate making charges as a percentage, a per-gram amount or using another pricing method. This calculator uses a percentage-based method.

Things to Check Before Buying Jewellery

  • Confirm the metal purity.
  • Check the weight mentioned on the invoice.
  • Check the metal rate being used.
  • Ask how wastage has been calculated.
  • Check the making charge.
  • Verify applicable taxes.

Important Information

This calculator does not determine the official market price of gold, silver, platinum or any other metal.

It also does not determine the legally applicable tax rate, purity, hallmarking requirements or a jeweller's pricing policy.

The actual invoice may differ from this estimate. Always verify the final price and applicable charges with the jeweller.
