SIP Calculator

Calculate the estimated future value of your monthly SIP investment based on your investment amount, expected annual return and investment period.

SIP returns are market-linked and not guaranteed. This calculator provides a mathematical estimate based on the expected annual return entered by you. Actual investment returns may be higher or lower.

What Is a SIP?

A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) allows an investor to invest a fixed amount at regular intervals, commonly every month, into a mutual fund or other investment product.

SIP investing can help investors invest regularly instead of investing a large amount all at once.

How to Use the SIP Calculator

  1. Enter your monthly SIP amount.
  2. Enter your expected annual return.
  3. Enter the number of years you plan to invest.
  4. Click Calculate.

The calculator will estimate your total investment, estimated returns and future value.

SIP Calculator Example

Suppose you invest ₹5,000 per month for 10 years and assume an annual return of 12%.

The calculator will show the approximate amount invested, estimated returns and total future value based on that assumption.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are SIP returns guaranteed?

No. SIP investments in market-linked products such as mutual funds do not guarantee a fixed return. The actual return depends on market performance.

What annual return should I enter?

Enter an assumed annual return for estimation purposes. You can compare different scenarios by trying different return percentages.

Can I use this calculator for different SIP amounts?

Yes. You can enter any reasonable monthly SIP amount and change it to compare different investment scenarios.

Does the calculator include taxes or fees?

No. This calculator provides a mathematical estimate and does not account for taxes, exit loads, fund expenses or other investment-specific charges.

Can I use this calculator for long-term investing?

Yes. You can enter longer investment periods to see how regular contributions and assumed returns may affect the estimated future value.
