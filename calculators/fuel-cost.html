Fuel Cost Calculator

Estimate how much fuel you need for a trip, the total fuel cost and the approximate cost per kilometre using your vehicle's mileage and current fuel price.

Fuel prices and actual vehicle mileage can vary. This calculator provides an estimate based on the values entered.

What Is Fuel Cost?

Fuel cost is the amount spent on petrol, diesel or another vehicle fuel during a journey. It depends mainly on the distance travelled, the vehicle's fuel efficiency and the price of fuel.

This calculator estimates fuel consumption using the vehicle's stated mileage in kilometres per litre.

Fuel Cost Formula

The estimated fuel required is calculated as:

Fuel Required = Distance ÷ Mileage

The estimated fuel cost is:

Fuel Cost = Fuel Required × Fuel Price

The approximate cost per kilometre is:

Cost per km = Fuel Cost ÷ Distance

Fuel Cost Calculation Example

Suppose your trip distance is 250 km, your vehicle gives a mileage of 18 km/litre and fuel costs ₹100 per litre.

Fuel Required = 250 ÷ 18 = 13.89 litres

Fuel Cost = 13.89 × ₹100 = ₹1,388.89

Approximate Cost per km = ₹5.56/km

How to Calculate Round-Trip Fuel Cost

If you are travelling to a destination and returning to your starting point, enter the total round-trip distance in the Trip Distance field.

For example, if the one-way distance is 125 km, the round-trip distance is 250 km. Enter 250 km into the calculator.

How to Use This Fuel Cost Calculator

  1. Enter the total distance of your trip in kilometres.
  2. Enter your vehicle's mileage in km/litre.
  3. Enter the current fuel price per litre.
  4. Click Calculate.
  5. View the estimated fuel required, fuel cost and cost per kilometre.

What Affects Vehicle Mileage?

  • Driving speed and acceleration
  • Traffic and road conditions
  • Vehicle load
  • Tyre pressure
  • Air-conditioning usage
  • Vehicle maintenance
  • Driving habits

The mileage shown by a manufacturer or vehicle dashboard may differ from real-world mileage.

Ways to Reduce Fuel Costs

  • Maintain steady speeds where road conditions allow.
  • Avoid unnecessary acceleration and hard braking.
  • Keep tyres properly inflated.
  • Remove unnecessary weight from the vehicle.
  • Maintain the vehicle according to the manufacturer's recommendations.
  • Plan routes to reduce unnecessary distance and traffic delays.

Important Information

This calculator provides an estimate based on the distance, mileage and fuel price entered by the user.

Actual fuel consumption can differ because of traffic, driving style, road conditions, weather, vehicle condition and other factors.

Fuel prices may also change by location and over time.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is fuel cost calculated?

Fuel required is calculated by dividing the trip distance by vehicle mileage. The fuel required is then multiplied by the fuel price per litre.

What does km/litre mean?

Km/litre means the approximate number of kilometres a vehicle can travel using one litre of fuel.

Can I calculate the cost of a round trip?

Yes. Enter the total distance for both the onward and return journeys.

Can I use this calculator for petrol and diesel vehicles?

Yes. Enter the appropriate fuel price and the vehicle's mileage.

Why is my actual fuel cost different?

Actual fuel consumption can differ from the mileage entered because of traffic, driving conditions, vehicle load, speed and other factors.
