Wedding Budget Calculator

Estimate your wedding budget by entering expected expenses for different categories.

This is an estimate based on the amounts you enter. Actual wedding costs depend on location, vendors, guest count, dates, services and personal preferences.

What Is a Wedding Budget Calculator?

A wedding budget calculator helps you estimate how much you may need to spend across major wedding categories.

It can help couples and families organise expected expenses before booking venues, vendors and other services.

Wedding Expenses Included

  • Venue and food
  • Decoration
  • Photography and video
  • Clothes and jewellery
  • Travel and accommodation
  • Miscellaneous expenses
  • Emergency or contingency reserve

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your estimated venue and food cost.
  2. Enter the expected decoration cost.
  3. Enter photography and video expenses.
  4. Enter clothing and jewellery expenses.
  5. Enter travel and accommodation costs.
  6. Add other expected expenses.
  7. Enter the number of guests if you want the per-guest estimate.
  8. Optionally add a contingency percentage.
  9. Click Calculate.

Why Keep a Contingency Budget?

Wedding expenses can change during planning. Additional guests, last-minute purchases, transportation changes, vendor upgrades or unexpected charges can increase the final cost.

Keeping a contingency reserve can provide some flexibility for unexpected expenses.

Wedding Budget Example

Suppose your estimated expenses are:

  • Venue / Food: ₹3,00,000
  • Decoration: ₹1,00,000
  • Photography: ₹80,000
  • Clothes / Jewellery: ₹1,50,000
  • Travel / Stay: ₹50,000
  • Miscellaneous: ₹50,000

Your base wedding budget would be ₹7,30,000.

If you keep a 10% contingency reserve, an additional amount of approximately ₹73,000 would be added.

The estimated budget including the reserve would therefore be ₹8,03,000.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this calculator give the actual wedding cost?

No. It provides an estimate based on the amounts you enter. Actual costs depend on your vendors, location, wedding date, guest count and choices.

What should I include in miscellaneous expenses?

You can include invitations, gifts, makeup, transportation, permits, small purchases and other expenses that do not fit into the main categories.

What is a contingency reserve?

A contingency reserve is extra money kept aside for unexpected or additional wedding expenses.

Can I use this calculator for a small wedding?

Yes. The calculator works for small, medium or large weddings. Simply enter the amounts that apply to your event.

Can I calculate the cost per guest?

Yes. Enter the number of guests and the calculator will estimate the average wedding budget per guest.

Is this Wedding Budget Calculator free?

Yes. The calculator is free to use.
