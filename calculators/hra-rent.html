Rent & HRA Planner Calculator

Estimate annual rent paid and a simple rent-to-income ratio; actual HRA exemption depends on tax rules.

This calculator provides a general estimate for planning and educational purposes. Verify important financial, tax, business or professional decisions with the relevant official source or qualified professional.

Formula

Annual rent = Monthly rent × 12

Example

₹20,000 monthly rent is ₹2.4 lakh per year.
