Bill Split Calculator

Split a restaurant, food or other shared bill between multiple people after adding tax, service charges and tip.

What Is a Bill Split Calculator?

A Bill Split Calculator helps you divide a shared bill between two or more people. It can include additional tax or service charges and a tip before calculating the final amount that each person needs to pay.

It is useful when friends, family members or colleagues share a restaurant, food, travel or other group expense.

How Does the Bill Split Calculator Work?

The calculator first calculates the tax or service charge and tip based on the original bill amount. These amounts are added to the bill to determine the total amount.

The final total is then divided equally by the number of people entered in the calculator.

Bill Splitting Formula

Tax / Service Charge = Bill Amount × Tax Rate ÷ 100

Tip = Entered Tip Amount

Total Bill = Bill Amount + Tax / Service Charge + Tip

Amount Per Person = Total Bill ÷ Number of People

Bill Split Example

Suppose four people have a restaurant bill of ₹2,000. The tax or service charge is 5% and the tip is 10%.

Tax / Service Charge = ₹2,000 × 5% = ₹100

Tip = ₹2,000 × 10% = ₹200

Total Bill = ₹2,000 + ₹100 + ₹200 = ₹2,300

Amount Per Person = ₹2,300 ÷ 4 = ₹575

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter the total bill amount.
  2. Enter the applicable tax or service charge percentage.
  3. Enter the tip amount, if applicable.
  4. Enter the number of people sharing the bill.
  5. Click Calculate.
  6. Check the total bill and amount payable per person.

Tips for Splitting a Shared Bill

  • Check whether taxes or service charges are already included in the bill.
  • Make sure the number of people is entered correctly.
  • If everyone ordered different items, an equal split may not be appropriate.
  • Round individual payments only after calculating the exact amount.
  • Always check the final restaurant or merchant bill before making payment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I split a bill between two people?

Yes. Enter 2 as the number of people and the calculator will divide the final bill equally between them.

Can I include a tip?

Yes. Enter the tip percentage and the calculator will add the calculated tip to the original bill.

Does the calculator include tax or service charges?

Yes. You can enter a percentage for tax or service charges and the calculator will add it to the bill before splitting the total.

Does everyone have to pay the same amount?

This calculator assumes an equal split. If people ordered different items or have different expenses, the actual amounts may need to be calculated separately.
