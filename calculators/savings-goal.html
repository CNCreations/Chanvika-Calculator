Savings Goal Calculator

Find out how much you need to save each month, week and day to reach your savings target.

This calculator does not include investment returns, interest, inflation, taxes or investment fees. It assumes that the remaining amount is saved equally over the selected period.

What Is a Savings Goal?

A savings goal is a specific amount of money you want to accumulate within a particular period.

Examples include saving for an emergency fund, wedding, education, vehicle, holiday, business equipment or a large purchase.

Savings Goal Formula

The amount still required to reach your goal is:

Amount Needed = Target Amount − Current Savings

The required monthly saving is:

Monthly Saving = Amount Needed ÷ Number of Months

This calculator also provides approximate weekly and daily targets based on the monthly requirement.

Savings Goal Example

Suppose your target is ₹1,00,000, you already have ₹10,000, and you want to reach the goal in 12 months.

Amount still needed: ₹90,000

Required monthly saving: ₹7,500

So you would need to save approximately ₹7,500 per month to reach the target in 12 months, assuming no investment returns or interest.

Track Your Savings Progress

Your current savings can be compared with your target to understand how much of the goal has already been completed.

For example, if your target is ₹1,00,000 and you already have ₹25,000, you have completed approximately 25% of your goal.

How to Reach a Savings Goal

  1. Set a specific target amount.
  2. Determine how much you already have saved.
  3. Choose a realistic deadline.
  4. Calculate the required monthly saving.
  5. Keep your savings separate from everyday spending.
  6. Review your progress regularly.

Tips for Reaching Your Savings Goal

  • Set an automatic savings amount if possible.
  • Track unnecessary expenses.
  • Save part of unexpected income.
  • Break a large goal into smaller monthly targets.
  • Review your target if your income or expenses change.
  • Keep an emergency fund separate from other savings goals.

Important Information

This calculator assumes that you simply save the required amount without earning interest or investment returns.

If your savings are invested and generate returns, the amount you actually need to contribute may be different.

Actual results depend on your savings behaviour, returns, fees, inflation and other financial factors.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much should I save every month?

It depends on your target amount, existing savings and deadline. This calculator estimates the monthly amount required based on those values.

Does this calculator include interest?

No. The basic calculation assumes that your savings do not earn interest or investment returns.

What if my current savings are already higher than my target?

The calculator will show that no additional amount is required to reach the target.

Can I use this calculator for a wedding or business goal?

Yes. You can use it for weddings, travel, education, emergency funds, business purchases and other savings goals.

Is this Savings Goal Calculator free?

Yes. You can use this calculator for free.
