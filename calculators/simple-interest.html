Simple Interest Calculator

Calculate simple interest and the total amount using the principal, annual interest rate and time period.

This calculator uses the standard simple interest formula: Interest = Principal × Rate × Time ÷ 100.

What Is Simple Interest?

Simple interest is interest calculated only on the original principal amount. Unlike compound interest, previously earned interest is not added to the principal for future interest calculations.

Simple Interest Formula

The standard formula is:

Simple Interest = (Principal × Rate × Time) ÷ 100

The total amount is:

Total Amount = Principal + Simple Interest

Simple Interest Example

Suppose you invest or lend ₹10,000 at an annual interest rate of 8% for 2 years.

Simple interest would be: ₹1,600

The total amount would therefore be: ₹11,600.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter the principal amount.
  2. Enter the annual interest rate.
  3. Enter the time period in years.
  4. Click Calculate.
  5. Review the interest and total amount.

Simple Interest vs Compound Interest

With simple interest, interest is calculated on the original principal. With compound interest, interest can also be earned on previously accumulated interest.

Therefore, compound interest can produce a different final amount, especially over longer periods.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the formula for simple interest?

Simple Interest = Principal × Rate × Time ÷ 100.

Does this calculator use annual interest rate?

Yes. Enter the interest rate as an annual percentage and the time in years.

Can I use this calculator for loans?

Yes, it can provide a basic simple-interest estimate. However, actual loan calculations may include fees, charges, different repayment structures or other terms.

What is the difference between interest and total amount?

Interest is the additional amount earned or charged. Total amount is the original principal plus the interest.
