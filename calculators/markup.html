Markup Calculator

Calculate the markup amount and estimated selling price using your cost price and desired markup percentage.

Markup is calculated as a percentage of cost price. Taxes, discounts, shipping and other business expenses are not included.

What Is Markup?

Markup is the amount added to the cost of a product or service to determine its selling price.

For example, if a product costs ₹500 and you apply a 30% markup, the markup amount is ₹150 and the estimated selling price is ₹650.

Markup Formula

The markup amount is calculated as:

Markup = Cost Price × Markup % ÷ 100

The selling price is:

Selling Price = Cost Price + Markup

Markup Calculation Example

Suppose a shop purchases a product for ₹500 and wants to apply a 30% markup.

Markup = ₹500 × 30% = ₹150

Selling Price = ₹500 + ₹150 = ₹650

Therefore, the estimated selling price before taxes, discounts and other charges would be ₹650.

Markup vs Profit Margin

Markup and profit margin are related but they are not the same thing.

Markup is calculated based on the cost price, while profit margin is calculated based on the selling price.

For example, if the cost is ₹500 and the selling price is ₹650:

  • Markup = ₹150 ÷ ₹500 × 100 = 30%
  • Profit Margin = ₹150 ÷ ₹650 × 100 ≈ 23.08%

So a 30% markup does not mean a 30% profit margin.

Why Is Markup Important for a Business?

Businesses often use markup to help set selling prices for products and services.

A suitable markup can help cover operating expenses and contribute towards profit. However, the correct markup depends on the business, product category, competition and other costs.

Costs to Consider Before Setting a Markup

A simple markup calculation only considers the cost price. A business may also have other expenses, such as:

  • Rent
  • Employee wages
  • Electricity
  • Packaging
  • Transportation
  • Payment processing fees
  • Marketing expenses
  • Taxes
  • Returns and damaged inventory

These expenses should be considered when deciding the final selling price.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is markup calculated?

Markup is calculated by multiplying the cost price by the markup percentage and dividing by 100.

What is the difference between markup and margin?

Markup is calculated as a percentage of cost price, while profit margin is calculated as a percentage of selling price.

What is a 30% markup on ₹500?

A 30% markup on ₹500 is ₹150, resulting in an estimated selling price of ₹650.

Does markup include GST?

No. This calculator calculates markup and selling price before GST or other taxes unless those costs are already included in your cost price.
