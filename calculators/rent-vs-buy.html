Rent vs Buy Calculator

Compare an estimated rent cost with the cost of buying an asset over a chosen period.

Results are estimates for general informational purposes and may differ from actual financial, tax, lender or business outcomes.

What Does This Calculator Do?

Rent vs Buy comparisons can help frame a housing or asset decision. This simplified calculator does not include every ownership or investment cost.

Formula

Estimated future purchase value = Purchase Price × (1 + appreciation rate)^years. Total rent = Monthly Rent × 12 × years.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter the required values.
  2. Check that your inputs are reasonable and use the correct units.
  3. Click Calculate to view the estimate.
  4. Adjust the inputs to compare different scenarios.

Example

Example: A ₹30 lakh purchase and ₹15,000 monthly rent over 10 years can be compared using your assumed appreciation rate.

Important Notes

Use this tool as a quick estimate. Actual results may depend on rates, taxes, fees, lender policies, market conditions, contracts, or other real-world factors.

Frequently Asked Questions

No. The calculator provides an estimate based on the information and assumptions you enter.

Yes. It can help compare scenarios and understand the effect of changing inputs before making a decision.

Should I verify the result?
