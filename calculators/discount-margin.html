Discount & Margin Calculator

Calculate the selling price after a discount and estimate the resulting profit margin from cost price.

Results are estimates for general informational purposes and may differ from actual financial, tax, lender or business outcomes.

What Does This Calculator Do?

This calculator combines discount pricing with cost information so you can see the final selling price and estimated margin.

Formula

Selling Price = Marked Price × (1 − Discount ÷ 100). Profit Margin = (Selling Price − Cost) ÷ Selling Price × 100.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter the required values.
  2. Check that your inputs are reasonable and use the correct units.
  3. Click Calculate to view the estimate.
  4. Adjust the inputs to compare different scenarios.

Example

Example: ₹1,000 marked price with 10% discount gives ₹900 selling price. With ₹500 cost, profit is ₹400 and margin is 44.44%.

Important Notes

Use this tool as a quick estimate. Actual results may depend on rates, taxes, fees, lender policies, market conditions, contracts, or other real-world factors.
