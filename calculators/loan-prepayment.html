Loan Prepayment Calculator

Estimate interest savings from making a lump-sum loan prepayment while keeping tenure assumptions simple.

This calculator provides a general estimate for planning and educational purposes. Verify important financial, tax, business or professional decisions with the relevant official source or qualified professional.

Formula

Compare interest on original principal vs principal after prepayment.

Example

A ₹1 lakh prepayment reduces the principal on which future interest is calculated.
