Unit Price Calculator

Calculate the price per unit of a product and compare different package sizes easily.

Use the same unit when comparing products. For example, compare grams with grams or litres with litres.

What Is Unit Price?

Unit price tells you how much a product costs for one unit of quantity, weight or volume.

It is especially useful when comparing different package sizes in shops and supermarkets.

Unit Price Formula

The basic formula is:

Unit Price = Total Price ÷ Quantity

For example, if a 2 kg product costs ₹250:

₹250 ÷ 2 = ₹125 per kg

Unit Price Example

Suppose Product A costs ₹200 for 2 kg.

Its price is: ₹100 per kg.

If another product costs ₹130 per kg, Product A is cheaper based on unit price.

Why Compare Unit Prices?

A larger package is not always cheaper. Comparing the unit price lets you see the actual cost of the product regardless of package size.

For example, a 500 g packet may appear cheaper than a 1 kg packet, but the 1 kg packet may provide better value when its price per kilogram is lower.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the cheapest way to compare products?

Compare the unit price rather than only the package price. The product with the lower comparable unit price generally costs less for the same quantity.

Can I compare different package sizes?

Yes. Unit pricing is specifically useful for comparing different package sizes.

Can I compare grams and kilograms directly?

Convert them to the same unit first. For example, compare ₹ per kilogram with ₹ per kilogram.

Can I use this calculator for liquids?

Yes. You can use it for millilitres or litres. Make sure the same unit is used when comparing products.
