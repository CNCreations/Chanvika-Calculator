Break-Even Sales Calculator

Estimate the sales revenue or units needed to cover fixed costs and reach break-even.

Results are estimates for general informational purposes and may differ from actual financial, tax, lender or business outcomes.

What Does This Calculator Do?

Break-even analysis estimates how many units you need to sell before your revenue covers fixed and variable costs.

Formula

Break-Even Units = Fixed Costs ÷ (Selling Price per Unit − Variable Cost per Unit).

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter the required values.
  2. Check that your inputs are reasonable and use the correct units.
  3. Click Calculate to view the estimate.
  4. Adjust the inputs to compare different scenarios.

Example

Example: ₹50,000 fixed costs with ₹500 selling price and ₹300 variable cost gives a contribution of ₹200 per unit, so break-even is 250 units.

Important Notes

Use this tool as a quick estimate. Actual results may depend on rates, taxes, fees, lender policies, market conditions, contracts, or other real-world factors.
