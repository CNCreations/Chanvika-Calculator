Loan Eligibility Calculator

Estimate the loan amount you may be able to afford based on income, existing EMI obligations, interest rate and loan tenure.

Results are estimates for general informational purposes and may differ from actual financial, tax, lender or business outcomes.

What Does This Calculator Do?

This calculator gives an indicative loan amount based on an assumed maximum EMI ratio. Actual eligibility depends on the lender, credit profile, income stability, age, documentation and other factors.

Formula

Loan Eligibility = Maximum Affordable EMI converted to principal using the standard EMI formula.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter the required values.
  2. Check that your inputs are reasonable and use the correct units.
  3. Click Calculate to view the estimate.
  4. Adjust the inputs to compare different scenarios.

Example

Example: With ₹50,000 monthly income, no existing EMI, 10% annual interest and a 5-year tenure, the calculator estimates an indicative maximum loan based on the assumed EMI ratio.

Important Notes

Use this tool as a quick estimate. Actual results may depend on rates, taxes, fees, lender policies, market conditions, contracts, or other real-world factors.
