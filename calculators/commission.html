Commission Calculator

Calculate commission earned on a sales or transaction value and find the amount remaining after commission.

What Is a Commission?

A commission is an amount paid or earned based on the value of a sale, transaction or service. It is commonly calculated as a percentage of the transaction value.

Commission-based payments are often used in sales, real estate, insurance, brokerage, affiliate sales and other businesses where earnings depend on completed transactions.

Commission Formula

The basic commission formula is:

Commission = Sales / Transaction Value × Commission Rate ÷ 100

The amount remaining after commission can be calculated as:

Amount After Commission = Sales Value − Commission

Commission Calculation Example

Suppose a salesperson completes a transaction worth ₹50,000 and the agreed commission rate is 5%.

Commission = ₹50,000 × 5% = ₹2,500

Amount After Commission = ₹50,000 − ₹2,500 = ₹47,500

Where Are Commission Calculations Used?

Important Things to Check

  • Confirm whether the commission rate applies to the full transaction value.
  • Check whether taxes or other charges are included in the commission calculation.
  • Make sure you are using the agreed commission percentage.
  • Some commission arrangements may have minimums, maximums or different rates for different sales levels.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is commission calculated?

Commission is commonly calculated by multiplying the sales or transaction value by the commission rate and dividing by 100.

What is a 5% commission on ₹50,000?

A 5% commission on ₹50,000 is ₹2,500.

Can I use this calculator for sales commissions?

Yes. You can use it to calculate a percentage-based commission on a sales or transaction value.

Does the calculator include taxes?

No. This calculator only calculates the commission based on the transaction value and commission rate. Any applicable taxes or other charges should be considered separately.

Can the commission rate be a decimal?

Yes. You can enter decimal commission rates such as 2.5% if applicable.
