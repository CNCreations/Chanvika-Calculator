Frequently Asked Questions

Are the calculators free?

Yes. The 101 calculators are designed to be free to use.

Do I need an account?

No account is required for the current calculators.

Is my calculation data saved?

The current version performs calculations in your browser and does not include a database for saving entries.

Can I use these calculators for business?

Yes, as quick estimation tools. For accounting, tax filing or significant financial decisions, verify figures with appropriate professional or official sources.
