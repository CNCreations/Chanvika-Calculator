Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 August 2026

This policy explains how Chanvika Calculator handles information on this website.

Information we collect

The current website does not require an account and does not intentionally collect calculator inputs into a database. Calculations are performed in the visitor’s browser.

Cookies and analytics

Chanvika Calculator uses Google Analytics (GA4) to understand aggregate website usage, such as pages viewed and general interaction with the site. Google may use cookies or similar technologies to provide measurement services. The site does not intentionally send calculator input values to a Chanvika database. If advertising, forms, payments, accounts or other third-party services are added, this policy should be updated.

Analytics choices

Visitors can manage or block cookies through their browser settings. Google Analytics data handling is also subject to Google's policies.

Third-party links

External websites have their own privacy practices. We are not responsible for their policies.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the website’s features change.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact method published on the Contact page.
