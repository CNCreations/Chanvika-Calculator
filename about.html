About Us

Chanvika Calculator is a simple, free web project focused on practical calculators for everyday life, shopping and small-business decisions in India.

Our goal

We want common calculations to be quick to understand and easy to use on a phone or laptop.

What you’ll find here

  • Business and pricing calculators
  • Money and finance estimation tools
  • Shopping and household calculators
  • Wedding and event planning helpers

Important

Our calculators provide estimates based on user-entered numbers. They are not a substitute for professional financial, tax, legal, medical or other expert advice.
